What needs work

  • Use a warmer greeting, empathetic nods, and open hand gestures.
  • Replace filler words such as “you know,” “right,” and “it’s like” with confident pauses.
  • Use the official branded uniform and a more polished grooming standard.
  • Correct the battery figure to 5,000 mAh and use the full Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy name.
  • Highlight the Built-in Privacy Display, Integrated S Pen, 60 W fast charging, and 7-year software guarantee.
