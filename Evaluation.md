## 4. Sales Methodology & Consultative Process

### 1. Discovery & Cross-Questioning (Rating: 2.0 / 5.0)
* **Gap:** When the customer explicitly stated: *"I use my phone pretty heavily throughout the day... What would you suggest?"*, the representative did not ask any qualifying questions.
* **Coaching Recommended:**
  * *"What specific tasks or applications make up most of your day—work emails, navigation, content creation, or multitasking?"*
  * *"Are you often in situations where battery anxiety or screen privacy in public spaces becomes an issue?"*

### 2. Objection Handling & Value Justification (Rating: 2.5 / 5.0)
* **Gap:** When the customer showed price hesitation (*"₹1.4 Lakh feels like a lot... not sure I need enough features to justify it"*), the representative immediately agreed and down-sold to the base Galaxy S26 without defending the Ultra's value.
* **Coaching Recommended:**
  * **Step 1 — Acknowledge & Validate:** *"I completely understand. It's a significant investment, so let's make sure every rupee delivers real value for your daily routine."*
  * **Step 2 — Connect Need to Differentiator:** *"Since you use your phone heavily all day, the 5,000 mAh battery with 60W charging and the redesigned vapor chamber prevent the lag and battery drain common in standard phones over 3-4 years."*
  * **Step 3 — Offer Affordability Pathways:** *"We also have direct exchange bonuses on your current phone and flexible 24-month no-cost EMI plans that bring the monthly cost down significantly."*
