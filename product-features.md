# Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra

The **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** is Samsung's flagship Galaxy S smartphone for 2026. It sits above the Galaxy S26 and Galaxy S26+ and is aimed at users who want Samsung's highest-end camera system, display, performance, productivity features, and AI capabilities.

Samsung launched the Galaxy S26 series in February 2026, with availability beginning in March 2026. In India, the Galaxy S26 Ultra launched at a starting price of **₹1,39,999**.

## Design and Build

The Galaxy S26 Ultra uses Samsung's premium Ultra design with a refined camera island and rounded ergonomic corners.

**Dimensions:** 163.6 × 78.1 × 7.9 mm
**Weight:** 214 g
**Frame:** Armor Aluminum
**Thickness:** 7.9 mm
**S Pen:** Integrated S Pen support

Samsung offers the phone in colors including:

* Cobalt Violet
* Black
* White
* Sky Blue
* Pink Gold — Samsung.com exclusive in some markets
* Silver Shadow — Samsung.com exclusive in some markets

Despite being a large flagship phone, Samsung reduced the device to 7.9 mm thick while retaining the 6.9-inch display and 5,000 mAh battery.

# Display

The S26 Ultra has a **6.9-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display**.

**Display specifications:**

* 6.9-inch / 17.49 cm panel
* Dynamic AMOLED 2X
* 3120 × 1440 QHD+ resolution
* Adaptive 1–120 Hz refresh rate
* Up to 2600 nits peak brightness
* 16 million color depth
* Anti-reflective display
* Built-in Privacy Display

The adaptive refresh rate means the screen can dynamically change its refresh rate up to 120 Hz depending on what you're doing.

For example, scrolling and gaming can run at high refresh rates while less dynamic content can use lower refresh rates to reduce power consumption.

## Privacy Display

One of the S26 Ultra's major new features is its **built-in Privacy Display**.

Instead of requiring a physical privacy screen protector, the display itself can reduce visibility when someone tries to look at the screen from an angle.

The user can apply privacy protection to:

* The entire display
* Notifications
* Selected applications

Looking directly at the phone keeps the screen visible, while viewing it from the side makes protected content considerably harder to see.

This is particularly useful when using the phone in airports, trains, offices or other crowded environments.

# Processor

The Galaxy S26 Ultra is powered by:

**Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy**

It is a customized version of Qualcomm's flagship processor optimized specifically for Samsung Galaxy devices.

The chip is manufactured using a **3 nm process**.

Samsung specifically emphasizes improvements to:

* CPU performance
* GPU performance
* NPU/AI performance
* Image processing
* Gaming
* Energy efficiency

Samsung says the customized processor delivers improved AI processing and significantly stronger NPU performance compared with the previous generation.

---

# Cooling System

Samsung redesigned the phone's vapor chamber cooling system.

The S26 Ultra provides approximately **21% greater thermal performance** according to Samsung.

The vapor chamber transfers heat away from components such as the CPU and GPU.

This matters during sustained workloads such as:

* Gaming
* Video recording
* Video editing
* AI processing
* Heavy multitasking
* Long camera sessions

Better thermal dissipation helps prevent the processor from reducing its clock speed because of excessive heat.

# Rear Camera System

Camera hardware is one of the main reasons Samsung positions this as the Ultra.

The S26 Ultra uses **four rear cameras**.

### 200 MP Main Camera

**Resolution:** 200 MP
**Aperture:** f/1.4
**Autofocus:** Yes
**OIS:** Yes

The wider f/1.4 aperture allows considerably more light into the main camera.

Samsung claims approximately **47% improved brightness** for the 200 MP wide camera compared with the previous implementation.

This particularly improves low-light photography and video.

### 50 MP Ultra-Wide Camera

**Resolution:** 50 MP
**Aperture:** f/1.9
**Autofocus:** Yes

The ultra-wide camera is intended for things such as:

* Landscapes
* Architecture
* Large groups
* Wide indoor shots
* Creative wide-angle photography

### 50 MP 5× Telephoto

**Resolution:** 50 MP
**Aperture:** f/2.9
**Optical zoom:** 5×

This is the primary long-range telephoto camera.

Samsung also provides **10× optical-quality zoom** using its Adaptive Pixel processing.

### 10 MP 3× Telephoto

**Resolution:** 10 MP
**Aperture:** f/2.4
**Optical zoom:** 3×

The 3× camera covers the intermediate telephoto range and is particularly useful for portraits and subjects that don't require the longer 5× lens.

### Complete Zoom System

The phone provides:

**2× optical-quality zoom**

**3× optical zoom**

**5× optical zoom**

**10× optical-quality zoom**

**Up to 100× digital zoom**

The 2× and 10× modes use Samsung's Adaptive Pixel system rather than separate 2× and 10× optical lenses.

You can perform operations such as:

* Remove objects
* Move objects
* Resize objects

# Circle to Search and AI Search

The S26 generation includes enhanced **Circle to Search** functionality.

The basic idea is that you can identify or search for something currently visible on the display without manually switching applications and typing a search query.

Samsung expanded this alongside its broader agentic AI functionality in the S26 generation.

---

# Agentic AI

Samsung describes the S26 generation as moving toward **agentic AI** rather than simply individual AI features.

The idea is that the phone can understand context and coordinate actions rather than requiring the user to manually execute every individual step.

The system combines Samsung's Galaxy AI capabilities with AI assistants and services available on the device.

This is one of the primary software differentiators Samsung is emphasizing for the S26 generation.
